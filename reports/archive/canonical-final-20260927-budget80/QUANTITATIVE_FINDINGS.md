# Quantitative findings

Mean ± sample SD across five independent optimization repeats; each test value averages ten noise seeds.

1. 1 kg, paired 80-evaluation bound comparison: mean minimum JT decreased from 0.00157706 to 0.00078041 rad (50.51%), while CT energy increased 13.40% (2.59846→2.94673 J). Decision: expanded bounds; expanded CT attached to its upper bound in 5/5 repeats.

2. At fixed selected bounds, extending the same candidate sequence from 40 to 80 evaluations reduced mean minimum JT by 1 kg: 1.05%; 2 kg: 10.63%; 3 kg: 17.43%.

3. On identical test seeds, transferred CT had lower JT in 10/10 heavy-payload comparisons, but only 8/10 transfers were feasible. All 8/8 feasible transfers beat the observed feasible local tracking extreme; infeasible transfers are excluded from this coverage-gap evidence.

4. Without retuning, CT at 3 kg had JT=0.0009673 ± 1.3301e-06 rad and JE=10.134 ± 0.0022272 J across all five repeats, including two current-limit violations; these are not five feasible outcomes. Changes from its own nominal response were 23.834 ± 0.059293% and 244.41 ± 0.32102%.

5. CE at 3 kg achieved JT=0.011664 ± 0.0079572 rad and JE=9.5221 ± 0.16161 J. Physical feasibility held in 43/45 representative conditions; JT≤0.05 rad alone held in 45/45, and both requirements held in 43/45. CT at 3 kg was feasible in 3/5 repeats. Zero PID was selected for 0/15 representative roles.
