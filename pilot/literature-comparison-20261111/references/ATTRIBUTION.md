# Original sources and derived extracts

The three PDF files in this directory are unchanged open-access source papers, credited in `downloaded_originals.json` with title, author, DOI, source URL and SHA256. Their original copyright/license statements remain inside each PDF. They are not newly produced research results. CC BY4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Text extraction was used locally for locating source passages; the published artifacts preserve the original PDFs and rendered evidence pages. The page PNGs are rendered derivatives used to check source tables. Consult the original PDF for equations and tables. Korean interpretation and model-consistency analysis are this study's commentary, not statements made by the original authors.
