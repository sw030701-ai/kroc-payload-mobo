# Frozen protocol (written before new simulations)
Exploratory seeds 101–103: 256 evaluations. Independent confirmatory seeds 201–210: 1024 evaluations. All four objectives, nominal/reduced finite actuator limits. Checkpoints 32,128,256,512,1024 where available. No post-result condition tuning. Conclusions conditional on this benchmark, not hardware.

Separate 2-DOF point-mass planar arm benchmark, not the repository canonical motor model. Eltayeb Table 2: l1=.2 m,l2=.1 m,m1=10 kg,m2=5 kg,g=9.81. Match mass matrix, initial zero state, training step [1,.5] rad, bounds Kp[0,200],Ki[0,50],Kd[0,10] per joint. Correct standard Coriolis second component to m2*l1*l2*sin(q2)*dq1² and gravity to cos(q1+q2). Printed source equations are physically inconsistent; numerical paper reproduction NOT claimed. No friction in nominal scenario; no payload or friction pivot.

PID torque=Kp*error+Ki*integral-Kd*velocity (derivative on measurement, avoids undefined step impulse). Conditional integration freezes a joint integrator when saturation and error drive farther outward. Controller dt=.002 s, RK4 plant substeps .001 s, held applied torque. T=10 s. Hard numerical failure |q|>20 rad, |dq|>200 rad/s, nonfinite: all objective scores 1e12, full candidate retained. Tracking feasibility: numerical completion AND both final errors <=.02 rad, reported separately (not optimization constraint).

Actuator nominal=[70,10] Nm, reduced=[42,6] Nm: rounded ~2x and ~1.2x analytical global static gravity bounds [34.335,4.905]. Finite nominal replaces ambiguous 'no limit'. These are benchmark design values, NOT actual motor ratings. Both exceed static gravity envelope; lower torque not chosen using observed objective effects. Infinite torque only sanity diagnostic.

Real-coded generational GA pop32, tournament2, uniform crossover probability .8, per-gene Gaussian mutation probability 1/6 (sd .1 normalized range), clipping bounds, elitist best-so-far archive; 32 new evaluations each generation; incumbent retained for reporting and parent pool (best replaces worst). Same RNG seed and initial normalized uniform population across objectives/limits; later candidates may differ through selection. Equal candidate budget; no hidden failed evaluation replacement. Original paper pop100/generations100/mutation .01 are NOT reproduced.

Each objective sums across joints: IAE/ISE/ITAE/ITSE, using left rectangle sampled t. Own scalar training minimum selects incumbent. Common metrics evaluate every selected gain without further selection on held-out constant step [.8,.7] and held-out quintic move to [1.2,.3] over 2 seconds. Test data never enter optimizer. RMSE=sqrt(sum joint error² / (2T)); IAE joint sum; settling both within .02rad until end (step only, censored NaN if never); maximum per-joint relative overshoot (step only); peak requested/applied torque, saturation joint-time fraction; failure and final-error feasibility.

Primary descriptive estimands: paired test RMSE differences IAE vs ITAE; objective×torque difference-in-differences; change of that contrast between budgets (three-way). Seed is statistical unit, trajectories separately; bootstrap paired seeds 10000 resamples, 95% percentile intervals, exploratory and confirmatory separated. Full six objective-pair contrasts included, no significance cherry picking; intervals unadjusted exploratory descriptions. Ten confirmatory seeds are evidence on this fixed setup, not broad novelty proof. Integration convergence checked selected gains at half controller timestep and half plant substep (combined sensitivity) and fixed controller timestep / refined plant (numerical). Exact rerun and model energy identities checked. No objective switch/pivot after looking at data.
