# Follow-up protocol frozen before new optimization
This is an exploratory redesign informed by PR2, not an independently preregistered discovery. Base 367544e. Only this new pilot directory is modified. Canonical model and main untouched.
Question: does mixed step+smooth training improve unseen smooth tracking at equal simulator rollout cost, and is the objective torque interaction stable after doubling bounds?
Eight NEW independent optimizer seeds 301–308; IAE/ISE chosen because prior supplementary contrast was large. Same corrected two-link point-mass physics, 10s horizon, PI-D, conditional integration, actual clipped applied torque, RK4 1ms internal/2ms controller, GA tournament/uniform crossover/Gaussian mutation as PR2. No original table-reproduction claim.
Factorial: 2 objectives x 2 torque limits x 2 gain bounds x 2 training schemes x 8 seeds =128 GA runs. Step candidate costs1 rollout, mixed candidate costs2 rollouts; aggregate objective is arithmetic mean over [step task0,smooth task2]. SAME total training simulator call budgets128/256; step explores twice as many gain candidates. This estimates allocation of a fixed rollout resource, not pure trajectory diversity holding candidate count equal. No uncounted replacement or duplicate caching. Shared normalized initial population and RNG seed; physical initialization differs with bounds. Mixed and step share physical initial points within bounds. Budgets are nested checkpoints, not independent runs.
Test NEVER used in selection: new smooth targets [.6,.9]/3s, [1,.6]/1s, [.9,.8]/4s; new step [.6,.9]. Start0, horizon10s, each smooth uses quintic with hold. Previously inspected smooth task2 is training here and no longer called test. Test group stays unexamined until all optimization completes. No early stopping by test.
Primary endpoint: mean RMSE across THREE new smooth tasks per optimizer seed, NOT trajectory samples as replicates. Four primary contrasts=mixed-step at final256rollouts for each objective x bounds, under reduced torque. Paired bootstrap10000 percentile95%CI, exact sign-flip with symmetry assumption and Holm over four contrasts. Nominal, torque/objective/bounds/budget interactions, step results and feasibility are secondary exploratory. Failures retained; failed final tests invalidate improvement claims and are reported, not dropped. No universal success threshold or automatic paper acceptance based on p value.
All candidate gains/scores and rollout components archived. Training score selection independently audited; initial populations and rollout accounting checked. Numerical refinement fixed-controller step for all final test gains/tasks. Literature overlap can lead to rejection even with favorable numerical differences. Bounds doubled globally as sensitivity, not hardware specification or claim of adequate search domain.
